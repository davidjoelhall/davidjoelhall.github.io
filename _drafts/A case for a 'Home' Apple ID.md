There are many devices that a person has. A person could have a single iPhone. A person could have an iPhone, Apple Watch, Mac, Apple TV, and AirPods. Now, imagine a group of people who live in the same house. Now, some of those devices are shared amongst the people who live in the house. 

There should be a way to have devices as part of a "Home" and not necessarily tied to a single person. 

An example:

An Apple TV. That he enjoyed by any number of people in the house. If I "forget" "my" AirPods from the TV, why do they get disconnected from my iCloud account? I just want to have them removed from this particular device. Maybe there should be a way to manage devices and connect them to separate pieces of the puzzle. AirPods connect to my iPhone and iPad, but never my Mac. Never automatically switch between devices.
